A clear, honest interface for your transfer inquiries

This platform centralises information about international transfer procedures between the United States and Vanuatu, and lets you submit a verification request. Each request receives a reference number so that its status can be tracked: received — pending verification.

How your request is processed

Four simple, transparent steps — from submission to confirmation with your bank.

1

Submit your request

Fill in the form : name, e-mail, country, amount and subject.

2

Receive your reference

A unique reference number is generated and displayed together with your summary page.

3

Pending verification

Your request is marked “received — pending verification”. The information will be reviewed.

Destination profile

Vanuatu, Republic of Vanuatu

An archipelago nation of the South Pacific, east of northern Australia. Transfers toward Vanuatu are usually made in Vatu (VUV) or in a major international currency, and may involve intermediary banks along the way.
